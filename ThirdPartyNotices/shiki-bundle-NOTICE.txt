Bundled-component notice for the code-highlight UI plugin
=========================================================

The file code-highlight.js inside this package (and in artifacts/plugins/
code-highlight.lspkg) is a minified esbuild bundle. Its entry point imports
shiki, and the bundle also contains the following upstream packages. The
minifier strips their source comments, so their copyright notices are listed
here instead.
